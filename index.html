FAQ

Questions people ask

Does ConfGoOne upload my configuration files?

No. Every check runs on your own PC. If you turn on the optional AI review, it only receives a redacted copy with passwords and keys removed first.

Which devices and systems can it audit?

Network devices such as Cisco IOS, IOS XE, NX-OS and ASA, Juniper, Fortinet FortiGate, Palo Alto, Check Point, SonicWall, MikroTik, Aruba, F5 BIG-IP and Huawei; Linux and Windows servers; Apache, Nginx and IIS; MySQL, PostgreSQL and SQL Server; AWS, Azure, Google Cloud, Kubernetes, Docker and Terraform; and ICS / OT systems.

Which compliance frameworks are covered?

Findings are mapped to CIS Benchmarks, DISA STIG, NIST SP 800-53, ISO/IEC 27001:2022, PCI-DSS, HIPAA, CMMC, NIST CSF and more. You can audit against one framework only — for example CIS — and the report covers just that framework.

Can it produce DISA STIG checklists?

Yes. ConfGoOne exports .ckl files for STIG Viewer 2 and .cklb files for STIG Viewer 3, with each STIG item filled in from the audit.

Do I need AI to use it?

No. The rule-based audit works without any AI. AI is optional — use your own provider or a free local model through Ollama.

Is there a free version?

Yes. The free plan includes three full audits with CIS and NIST 800-53 mapping and HTML reports.
