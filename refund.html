Refund Policy

Try it for free before you ever pay — that's the whole point of the Free tier. Because of that, here's our stance on refunds once you do purchase.

Try before you buy: the Free tier gives you 3 real scans, with no time limit, using the full deterministic review engine — enough to genuinely evaluate whether ConfGoOne works for you before spending anything.

Our policy

Because the Free tier already lets you fully evaluate the product before purchasing, we do not offer refunds once a Pro or Enterprise license key has been issued. This applies to all billing terms — monthly, 6-month, and yearly.

Exceptions

We'll consider a refund on a case-by-case basis if:

  • You were charged in error (e.g. a duplicate charge, or a payment that failed but was still billed)
  • The software genuinely doesn't run on your system due to a bug on our end, and we're unable to fix it within a reasonable time
  • Any other exceptional circumstance you believe is worth raising with us

To request one, email confgoone@gmail.com with your order details and the reason. We'll respond within a few business days.

Cancelling a subscription

For monthly, 6-month, or yearly terms, you can cancel at any time to stop future renewal — your license stays active for the remainder of the term you already paid for, and simply won't renew afterward. There's no partial refund for the unused portion of a current term, consistent with the policy above.

How refunds are processed

If a refund is approved, it's issued back to the original payment method through Razorpay, our payment processor. Processing time depends on your bank or card issuer, typically 5–10 business days.

Contact

Questions about this policy, or a specific order: confgoone@gmail.com.
